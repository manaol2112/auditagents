# Lumen Health Partners Information Security Policy

Approved by the CFO. Version 2, effective 1 July 2025. Fictional.

1. Access to financial systems is granted on the basis of job need and must be approved by the employee's manager or the system owner before it is provisioned.

2. Managers review the access of their staff to financial systems at least twice a year. Access that is no longer required is removed within ten business days of the review.

3. Changes to financial systems are tested and approved before they reach production. Developers must not deploy their own changes to production unless a second person reviews the deployment.

4. Passwords for financial systems are at least twelve characters and multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access.
