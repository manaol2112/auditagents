# Optro: Platform Identity, History, Modules, and SOX/RCM Capabilities (as of November 2026)

## Who makes Optro, when was it launched or renamed, and what was it called before?

### Takeaway
Optro is the new name of AuditBoard, not a separate or successor vendor. AuditBoard announced the rebrand on **March 9, 2026** at the IIA's Great Audit Minds conference in Las Vegas. The company started in 2014 as **SOXHUB**, renamed itself AuditBoard in 2017, and has been owned by the private equity firm **Hg** since a deal announced in May 2024 that valued it at more than $3B. The vendor describes the rebrand as "same company, same products, same customers."

## What modules/products does it have, and which ones hold the RCM (processes, risks, controls)?

### Takeaway
Optro currently lists 11 products on its "GRC Intelligence Platform." The SOX/ICFR RCM lives in **Controls Management**, the renamed or umbrella successor of the SOXHUB SOX module (product screenshots are still labelled "SOXHUB"). Autonomous Testing (from Midship) plugs into it. IT and framework controls sit in CrossComply, and enterprise risks in RiskOversight.

### Cited Findings
- The vendor lists 11 products: Controls Management; Autonomous Testing (powered by Midship); AI Governance (built on FairNow); OpsAudit (internal audit management); BCM (business continuity); CrossComply (IT compliance management); RiskOversight (enterprise/operational risk); Cyber Risk Management; RegComply (regulatory compliance); TPRM (third-party risk); Agentic Compliance. The platform is branded the "GRC Intelligence Platform." — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- The site navigation shows the same product list, plus platform items: Analytics, Optro AI, Services & Support, Integrations & APIs, Frameworks & Controls, Reporting & Dashboards. — [Optro rebrand blog](https://optro.ai/blog/meet-optro-auditboard-unveils-new-identity-as-ai-transforms-grc)
- Controls Management covers SOX, FDICIA, MAR, J-SOX, and the UK Corporate Governance Code. — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- The "SOX Management Software | Optro" page shows a dashboard image labelled "SOXHUB" and links to Controls Management in its product list. — [Optro SOX management page](https://optro.ai/product/sox-management)
- Optro's OOTB SOX RCM template page calls SOXHUB "Optro's SOX management solution." The template is access-restricted: "you must be a current Optro Customer." — [Optro Exchange: OOTB SOX RCM](https://optro.ai/exchange/template-ootb-sox-rcm)
- Midship is to be "fully integrated into Optro's Controls Management solution." Per coverage, this complements recently announced AI Narratives, Fieldwork Automations, and Continuous Control Monitoring. — [TechIntelPro](https://techintelpro.com/news/ai/robotics-automation/optro-acquires-midship-to-deliver-agentic-sox-automation-for-grc); [Pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/finance/news/optro-acquires-sox-automation-platform-midship-362c3805-70cd-42d4-bd01-76205a597745/)
- 30+ preloaded frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF, NIST 800-53 r5, PCI DSS 4.0, GDPR, HIPAA, SOX) come with Secure Controls Framework (SCF) common-control mappings. These are the domain of CrossComply and "Frameworks & Controls." — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)

### Inferences
- The SOX/ICFR RCM (processes or sub-processes, risks, controls, and test attributes) most likely sits in **Controls Management (SOXHUB)**. ITGCs might sit there too if the client tests them as part of SOX, or in **CrossComply** if they are run through a framework such as SOC 2 or NIST. Which one a client uses is a configuration choice, and only the client's own export can confirm it.
- The renaming is layered: SOXHUB (2014 company and product) → AuditBoard SOXHUB module → Optro "Controls Management" (2026 marketing name). The UI may still show "SOXHUB," so treat "SOXHUB," "AuditBoard SOX," and "Optro Controls Management" as the same module.

### Gaps
- No public documentation of the Controls Management data model (object hierarchy, field names, whether it has accounts, assertions, or locations objects) was found. The OOTB RCM template's columns are behind a customer login.
- Whether "Controls Management" fully replaces the "SOXHUB" product name or is a broader umbrella is not stated by the vendor.

## What does the vendor say about its SOX/ICFR, controls testing, and RCM functionality?

### Takeaway
The vendor's marketing centers on an out-of-the-box SOX RCM, AI-generated narratives and flowcharts mapped to risks and controls, continuous control testing, and AI agents running attribute tests on evidence (Midship). It also mentions certification workflows for SOX 302/404 and collaboration with external auditors. The public pages do not mention "ICFR," walkthroughs, scoping, or test-of-design vs. operating-effectiveness terminology.

### Cited Findings
- "Tackle SOX compliance — with embedded AI, all within a single, connected risk platform." — [Optro SOX management page](https://optro.ai/product/sox-management)
- "Out-of-the-box (OOTB) SOX RCM simplifies the process of establishing your initial program." — [Optro SOX management page](https://optro.ai/product/sox-management)
- "AI-generated narratives and flowcharts map in risks and controls, giving you complete context without the manual effort." — [Optro SOX management page](https://optro.ai/product/sox-management)
- Testing claims: "AI agents independently run attribute tests across uploaded evidence," with each step documented. The page also says "Surface deficiencies sooner," and that workflows and permissions "enable both the first line and external auditors to execute tasks independently." — [Optro SOX management page](https://optro.ai/product/sox-management)
- Continuous control testing "surfaces deficiencies as they happen." Autonomous Testing has AI agents run attribute tests (e.g., access reviews, bank reconciliations) on raw evidence. Automated certification workflows link management attestations to audit evidence for SOX 302 and 404. Pre-IPO companies use it 12–18 months ahead for SOX readiness. — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- The OOTB SOX RCM is "a sample out-of-the-box (OOTB) SOX RCM to help you get started." It is "pre-loaded," and teams can "easily tailor your SOX risks and controls," reducing "manual documentation and spreadsheet tracking for SOX controls." — [Optro Exchange: OOTB SOX RCM](https://optro.ai/exchange/template-ootb-sox-rcm)
- Customer claims (vendor-reported): 50+ public companies use Autonomous Testing; Reddit's internal audit team automated 150+ SOX controls in 90 days with Midship (Feb 2026). — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- Midship "up to an 87% reduction in manual effort" in SOX program management (projection). — [Infor Capital](https://inforcapital.com/news/optro-acquires-ai-native-sox-automation-platform-midship-to-advance-agentic-audit-transformation/)

### Inferences
- The marketing language ("map in risks and controls," "SOX RCM," "attribute tests," "deficiencies") suggests the RCM objects include at least risks, controls, narratives or flowcharts tied to processes, test attributes, and deficiencies or issues. This is inferred from marketing copy, not from a documented schema.

### Gaps
- The public pages do not mention ICFR, walkthroughs, scoping or materiality, financial statement assertions, or account-to-control mapping. I found no vendor statement either way on these RCM elements.
- I found no public release notes describing RCM field changes.

## Is there public help-center / knowledge-base / developer (API) documentation, and where?

### Takeaway
The help center is still on the legacy SOXHUB domain (`support.soxhub.com/hc/en-us`) and appears to need a login (403 to an anonymous fetch). Optro advertises a REST API, 200+ integrations, and an MCP server for AI tools, but **no public API reference was found**. Third parties report that the API docs are gated behind customer support.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor-listed resources: Product Support at support.soxhub.com/hc/en-us; Trust Center at trust.optro.ai; Community Hub at community.optro.ai; Optro Academy for training and certifications; integrations at optro.ai/integrations. — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- Vendor-documented integrations: 200+ out of the box (Workday, Okta, Oracle, Snowflake, SAP Concur, SAP SuccessFactors, CMDBs, vulnerability scanners, Jira), plus a "REST API for custom connections." An **MCP server** connects approved AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) to live Optro data under existing user permissions and can query "control matrices." — [Optro AI info page](https://optro.ai/ai-info-for-llms)
- Third-party: "AuditBoard API documentation is not publicly available… contact your AuditBoard support representative." Reported authentication is basic auth or bearer token; the API has been used for Power BI and Snowflake integrations. — [Supergood AuditBoard API page](https://supergood.ai/docs/auditboard-api); [Supergood API report card](https://supergood.ai/api-report-card/auditboard)
- Axonius's AuditBoard connector uses OAuth 2.0 client credentials with an API client that has the `action.view` permission scope. — [Axonius docs](https://docs.axonius.com/docs/audit-board)
- An anonymous fetch of support.soxhub.com/hc/en-us returned HTTP 403 (this session's observation).

### Inferences
- The help center runs on Zendesk (the `/hc/en-us` path is Zendesk's), and articles are probably customer-only. Field-level RCM documentation will likely have to come from the client's admin, their exports, or Optro support, not public web sources.

### Gaps
- No public API endpoint reference for controls, risks, or processes was found. The Optro MCP server's tool schema is not public in what I retrieved.
- I could not confirm whether the help center has moved, or will move, to an optro.ai domain.
